# 📖 SPED Teacher Guide: Choosing IEP Versions in Student Records

This guide explains how Special Education (SPED) teachers can view, compare, and export/print specific versions of a student's Individualized Education Program (IEP) in the **Student Records** module.

---

## 📌 Overview

A student's IEP evolves across quarters and school years as new assessments are conducted and updated goals are created. In **View Student Records**, teachers have complete control over which **Section B** (Difficulties, Barriers, and Enabling Supports) and **Section C** (Learner Goals & Quarterly Milestones) versions are displayed on screen and included in the printed/exported record.

---

## 🛠️ How to Choose Versions for View and Print

1. **Open Student Records:**
   - From the sidebar or dashboard, click **Student Records**.
   - Select the student from the list to open their complete multi-step record.

2. **Navigate to Step 3 (Section B & C):**
   - Review **Section A** (Personal Information & Diagnosis) and **Present Levels** (Academic & Functional Performance).
   - Click **Next →** to reach **Section B & C**.

3. **Select Section B Version:**
   - In the **Section B** header, locate the **Section B Version** dropdown.
   - Choose the version you want to view. By default, the system selects the **Latest** version.
   - The table immediately updates to showcase the selected version's difficulties, learning barriers, learning facilitators, and accommodations.

4. **Select Section C Version:**
   - In the **Section C** header, locate the **Section C Version** dropdown.
   - Choose the version of the learner goals you want to review.
   - The goal cards and 3-month milestone progression tables immediately update to reflect your choice.

5. **Print or Export to PDF:**
   - **EXPORT PDF:** Downloads the official DepEd-standard PDF report matching your chosen Section B and Section C versions directly to your computer.
   - **PRINT RECORD:** Opens your browser's print dialog to print the on-screen preview with your chosen versions, with clean formatting that hides screen buttons and navigation menus.

---

## 💡 Help & Troubleshooting

- **Session Preservation:** The system automatically remembers your chosen versions while you browse other steps or students during your active session.
- **Empty Goals / Version Not Found:** If a newly created or older version has no goals recorded, a message will prompt you to generate IEP goals or switch to an alternate version.
